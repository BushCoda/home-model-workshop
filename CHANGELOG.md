# Changelog

## Unreleased — 0.1.0 Beta 1

Planned initial public beta of Home Model Workshop. This entry will be dated when the release is approved and published.

- Guided room selection and separate room-footprint placement.
- Box, L, H, and U visual placement guides.
- Custom room definitions and imported room layouts.
- Overlap validation, open-gap behavior, and shared-wall generation.
- Door and window opening frames with rebuild-based cutouts.
- Collapsible panel sections and user/developer documentation.

This is a beta release. Room arrangement, walls, and openings should be reviewed in Blender before relying on the generated model.
